Peanut Butter Pickle Sandwich

home

To make one serving, you'll need

    • 2 slices of sandwich bread
      8 to 12 dill pickle chips (enough to cover the bread)
      peanut butter

    • Making the sandwich

    • 1. lay out the pickle chips on paper towels to dry so it's juices won't get the bread soggy
      2. use a small knife or a tablespoon to scoop out the peanut butter and smear it all across the bread
      3. then lay the dry picke chips on the peanut butter slathered bread and lay the other slice on top

    • now you have a safe, and awesome sandwich fit for GREATNESS!
